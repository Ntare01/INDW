About us

Keepers of a tradition that refuses to fade.

Indinzi means "guardians" — and that's exactly what we are: guardians of the dances, drumbeats and stories that shaped generations before us.

Our story

Started with a rehearsal space and a refusal to forget.

Indinzi began as a small group of friends who met after school to practise the dances their grandparents once performed. What started informally grew into a full troupe: dancers, drummers and storytellers rehearsing weekly, performing across Kigali, and eventually teaching the next generation.

Today Indinzi performs at national celebrations, weddings, schools and festivals — always with the same goal: make sure the culture is not just remembered, but actively lived.

What guides us

Our values

Authenticity

Every dance, rhythm and story we perform is rooted in real tradition, taught by those who came before us.

Mentorship

We train young dancers and drummers year-round, so the tradition always has hands to carry it forward.

Community

Our performances are for everyone — schools, weddings, festivals — culture belongs in daily life, not just on stage.

Our journey

Milestones

2014

Indinzi is founded

A small group begins weekly rehearsals in a community hall in Kigali.

2017

First national stage

Indinzi performs at a national heritage celebration for the first time, in front of thousands.

2020

Youth training program launched

A dedicated program begins teaching dance and drumming to students after school.

2024

50+ performances a year

Indinzi now performs regularly at weddings, festivals, schools and corporate events across the country.

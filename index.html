Who we are

More than a performance — a living archive.

Founded by dancers, drummers and storytellers who refused to let tradition fade, Indinzi trains new generations while performing at festivals, weddings, schools and national celebrations across Rwanda. Every step, beat and verse is passed down with intention — and shared with anyone willing to listen.

What we carry

Three traditions, one troupe.

Dance

Intore & ceremonial dance

High-energy, precise movement passed down through decades of practice — performed with pride at every event we take part in.

Drum

Ingoma drumming

The heartbeat of every performance. Our drummers train for years to master the rhythms that carry each dance and story.

Story

Oral storytelling

Folktales, proverbs and praise poetry woven through performances — keeping language and memory alive for younger audiences.
